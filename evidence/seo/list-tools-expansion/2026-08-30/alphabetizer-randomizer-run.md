## SERP overlap

The runner produced a coherent Alphabetizer cluster containing `alphabetizer`, `alphabetizer online` and `list alphabetizer`, plus a closely related `alphabetical sorter` / `alphabetical sorter online` cluster. This supports one canonical Alphabetizer page rather than synonym pages.

Randomizer terms also clustered into overlapping `list randomizer / list shuffler / random list generator / random order generator` and `randomize a list / randomize list / shuffle a list` families. `randomize names` was more isolated and overlapped picker/wheel/name-specific products, so it is not the primary positioning target.

## Representative enriched pages

Alphabetizer/search competitors included `wordcounter.net/alphabetize`, `alphabetizer.flap.tv`, `alphabetizer.org`, `onlinetoolz.net`, `studycrumb.com` and `sortmylist.com`. Their page language repeatedly describes the job as alphabetizing/sorting a pasted list into alphabetical order.

Randomizer/search competitors included `random.org/lists`, `folge.me/tools/list-randomizer`, `randraw.com/online-draw`, `pinetools.com/list-randomizer` and `gigacalculator.com/randomizers/randomizer.php`. The SERP also contains stronger picker/wheel and programming pages for some adjacent queries, reinforcing the scope boundary around ordinary list shuffling rather than name generation or selection wheels.

A recently registered low-authority entrant, `letsrandomize.org`, surfaced in the enriched Randomizer cohort. This is possibility evidence, not proof of repeatable fast ranking.

## Implementation use

Use this evidence to keep one `/alphabetize-list` page for the Alphabetizer family and one later `/randomize-list` page for the shuffle/random-order family. For the Alphabetizer, preserve clear on-page language around “alphabetize a list”, “alphabetical order”, and A–Z/Z–A sorting without keyword-stuffed synonym sections.

Do not infer guaranteed rankings, traffic, revenue, exact US volume or a probability of SEO success from this snapshot.
