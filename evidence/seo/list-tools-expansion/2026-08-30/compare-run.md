## SERP / competitor evidence

Repeated specialist entrants included:

- `comparetwolists.com` — “Compare two lists - easy online listdiff tool”; repeatedly ranked across Compare/List Difference terms and describes unique/shared values;
- `difflists.com` — “DiffLists.com - compare lists online tool”; repeatedly ranked across the overlapping family;
- `listdiff.com` — repeated across several comparison/difference variants;
- `ipvoid.com/compare-two-lists/` — appears for explicit compare-two-lists intent.

The same specialist pages recur across `compare lists`, `compare two lists`, `list comparison`, `list compare`, `list difference` and `list diff`, supporting the existing decision that these variants belong to the canonical homepage rather than separate SEO pages.

Autocomplete also contains adjacent Excel, Python and Instagram formulations. Those are not evidence to create separate pages inside this expansion; the current browser list-comparison job remains the anchor.

Recent registrations appeared in the wider SERP cohort, but first-seen evidence was unavailable, so registration age alone must not be treated as ranking-speed proof.
